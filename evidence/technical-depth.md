## Identify at least two substantial computational techniques your solution may require and explain why each technique is necessary rather than decorative.
* Object Oriented Programming makes managing the volunteer side vs. the organizer side of the program much easier. It is necessary to make the code readable and efficient as it makes storing information much easier.
* Having multiple user roles is necessary as we don't want volunteers and organizers to see the exact same display. Volunteers shouldn't have access to all the internal records while organizers need this information.
## Identify one technical risk and your first plan for investigating it.
One technical risk is potential errors that allow people to access the data of other people or organizations. I will investigate various security features of other account based websites to see how they store data such that the program can use it, but it can't be accessed otherwise.
